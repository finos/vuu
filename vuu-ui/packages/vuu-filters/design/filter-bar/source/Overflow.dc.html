1 · Overflow menu: “+N”

Recommended base

Pills that don’t fit go behind a “+N” button at the end of the row, just before +. A dot on the button means at least one hidden filter is applied. The menu lists the hidden filters; the square shows which are applied. Enter toggles one, → opens its usual pill menu (Edit, Rename, Close, Delete).

2 · Shrink first, then overflow

Recommended addition

Before anything moves into “+N”, long labels truncate down to a minimum width (about 76px), with the full name in the tooltip. Kept: the rename input and the editing pill always get their full width.

3 · Applied filters stay visible

Option

A variant of 1: when overflowing, unapplied pills go into “+N” first, so applied filters stay on the row. Pills keep their relative order, but the gaps where unapplied ones were close up.

Editing while overflowing

Applies to 1–3

The editor pill always gets the space it needs: neighbours move into “+N” while it’s open and come back on Save or Cancel. Editing a filter from the “+N” menu opens it at the end of the row. If the clauses themselves don’t fit, they scroll inside the editor, keeping the focused clause in view and Save and Cancel pinned.

Suggested combination
  1. Shrink long labels to a minimum width (2).
  2. Then move pills from the end of the row into “+N”, keeping their order (1). A dot shows a hidden filter is applied.
  3. The pill being edited or renamed is never hidden or shrunk.
